    How it moves

    • The liquid churns hardest around the hands, most of all at their tips.
    • In the last ten seconds of every minute it builds, peaks as the minute turns, then settles.
    • Every hour, a drop of ink slowly swells in the liquid somewhere new, wobbling like a bubble, then bursts like thick paint, and the swirl slowly takes it back. At 1 am it's small; each hour it grows a little, building towards midnight. Hourly bursts, under Moments in Settings, turns this off.
    • Midnight is different. For ten minutes before, the swirl spreads across the whole screen. Through the last minute, volleys of ink in three shades come faster and faster. At 12:00 they flood the screen to black, and the clock slowly finds its way back over the next minute. On New Year's, the last ten seconds count down, and after the black, colour breaks through, each volley in shades of one colour.
    • Dawn in Paris is different again, wherever you are. The liquid builds three times, each with a volley of bursts of light, each one cut short. Then one great drop of light swells in the middle and bursts, flooding the screen white as the sun comes up, gives way to a long wash of orange morning light, and the clock slowly finds its way back over the next minute.
    • Second hand, under Clock in Settings, adds a thin second hand in the palette's tips colour. It sweeps smoothly and leaves a faint wake in the liquid.
    • Depth, under Liquid in Settings, lifts the hands off the liquid. Tilt the phone and they shift over it with their shadow. Deep also turns the colours into a raised, glossy surface that catches the light as you tilt. On a computer, move the mouse instead.
    • Touch the centre of the clock to open Settings and About. Nothing else is a button.

    Keeping it on display

    Added to your Home Screen or Dock, the clock opens full screen, without the browser's bars. Turn the phone sideways on a stand.

    After your first touch the clock asks the screen to stay on. If your phone still dims, set Auto-Lock (iPhone) or Screen timeout (Android) to a longer time while the clock is on display.

    Once it has opened, the clock keeps working without a connection.

    If your phone is set to reduce motion, the liquid stays calmer, the big moments are gentler, and Depth holds still.
